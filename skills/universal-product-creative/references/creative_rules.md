# Creative direction

Design product/buyer/campaign goal/mood/reference language/layout/scene/density/sequence separately. Category does not prescribe style. Select useful purposes rather than a mandatory six-card template. Unknown quantities or performance are omitted.

Only consumer-facing product descriptions belong in pictures. Project development, Pipeline, Skill, Agent, Mask, Evidence, source-pixel preservation, authenticity review and test progress belong in reports unless explicitly requested on the picture.

Vary scale, whitespace, hierarchy and negative space. Avoid repeating left-title/right-product cards or white panels on every environment. Explain composition choices. Numeric layout differences do not establish taste.
